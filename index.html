WELCOME TO SCRUBJAY

Scrubjay is a drag-and-drop bookmarking, game-making, and wandering tool, made by Everest Pipkin.

Scrubjay is a messy and experimental website builder. It is in beta and may never leave it.

Scrubjay works by scraping html blocks and hotlinking media from other websites. To begin, drag something (anything!) onto the page– or double click this object. Once you are ready to share, export your HTML and host it where your friends can see (or import it later to keep working).

You can read more about Scrubjay at everestpipkin.itch.io/scrubjay.
